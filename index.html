PROJECTION MODEL · SCALING SIGNALS
The Scaling Signals use a forward-looking model to project D3/D7/ROAS from current CPL + D1% attendance.
Ratios are auto-calculated from the last 4 mature weeks of actual data per campaign.
D1% ASSUMPTION
% of leads who attend Day 1.
Used as base for D3/D7 projection.
Proj D3 Cost = CPL ÷ (D1% × D3/D1 ratio)  ·  Proj D7 Cost = CPL ÷ (D1% × D7/D1 ratio)  ·  Proj ROAS = k ÷ Proj D7 Cost
